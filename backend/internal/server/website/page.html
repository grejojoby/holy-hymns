A FEW HELPFUL NOTES

Make yourself
at home.

Can I read the lyrics in Manglish?

Yes. Hymns with a Manglish version let you follow Malayalam words in the Latin alphabet. Switch between the available scripts on a hymn’s page.

How do I find a song I remember?

Search the collection using a title or a line from the lyrics. You can search in Malayalam or Manglish, then open a hymn to read the full lyrics.

Do I need an account to read lyrics?

No account is needed to browse or read. Open My collection to sign in and keep your saved hymns together.
