Thiruvosthi than thoovenmayil

Agathanaagunna thampuraane

Thirubhojyamaay agathaaril nee

Anandameguvaan vanneedane

Vanneedane eeshoye vanneedane

Ennullil ennum nee vanneedane

Kaarunyamaay chaithanyamaay

Aliverum snehame vanneedane

Ee loka saubhaagyavum

swargeeyananmakalum

vishwaasapoorna jeevithavum

aathmeeya saanthvanavum

enneshu naadhaa nee maathramennum

nalkunnu ennil ennolavum

Ee janma mohangalum

enaathma daahangalum

kannuneerin yaamangalum

Ekaantha bhaavangalum

en jeeva naadhaa nee maathram

ennum kaanunu nithyam aaradhyame

Thiruvosthi....
