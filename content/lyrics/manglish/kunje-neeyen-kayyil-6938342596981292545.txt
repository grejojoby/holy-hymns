Kunje neeyen kayyil chanjadumbol

Naam kannil kannil nokki kaliyadumbol

Eesho vanneedunnoo nammodonnakunnu

Ninne kayyelkkunnu umma thanneedunnu

Swargan ennullil vannallo raareero

Ente punnara thankame vaavaavo

Kanneerode janmam nalki

Ente kunjavayayi nee kanda naakl

Enthanandam Anna neram

Daivam sammanam thannoru paithale

Kaalam ozhukumbol ninnil eeshoyum valarumallo

Njan eattavum bhagyavathee

Ennayalum enne piriyum nee

Mannil nalloru nilayil uyarum nee

Ennanandam mannil eesho

Thante santhosham nalki jeevikkum

Lokam vilikkumbol ninte Daivathe maranneedalle

Ninnashrayam ennumavan
