Ella neravum sthuthi paadum

aathmaavil vaazhunna eeshoyku (2)

altharayil varum thiru naadhanu

appamai theerunna guru naadhanu (2)

aaradhanayude pon chirakil

aathmaavil alinju njan parannuyarum

maalaakhamar chernnu paadum

halelluia... halelluia...

ullonnu thurannal ullil vasikkan

aathma vaathilil vannu muttunna neram (2)

aa divya swaram kett aathmaharshathode

en ponneeshoye njan sweekarikkan (2)

aaradhanayude pon chirakil...

aathmavin vediyil althara theerkaan

althaarayilenne beliyay nalkan (2)

aa sneha beli njan poorthiyakum neram

nin ponkireedam njan sweekarikam (2)

ella neravum sthuthi paadum...

aaradhanayude pon chirakil...
