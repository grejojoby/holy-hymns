Kallinmel veenu ninte pulloori muttumpotti

Vallathe muripetti-ttellukal velipettu

Vellapoonthudakalil kollichoradikalaal

Thullippoy tholum maamsamellolamidiyiila

Muttaadin tholurinju vittonam ninte nenjin

Kottayum Tholuriyapettappol kaanunnallo

Paksham niranja ninte vakshassum njangal paapa

Shikshakkaay thurannittum pakshaththe kaattunallo

Datham kadanjapole chantham thulumbum kaikal

Kuntham polaaniyettu chinthunnu rakthamettam

Kaikkanakkillathe njan cheytha paapangal ninte

Kaikale kurishinmel ayyo tharacheevannam

Kundthidham chankinekum Kandtavum dushtarkkulla

Kandakhanaghangale kondettam murivettu

Vaanavar kanninetta maanandamerum ninte

Aananamadikalal thane nilachuvingi

Mannil thuppi kurudar kannukal thelicha nin

Kannilum thuppi yoodhar danddhippichettam ninne

Kuppayepole neechar thuppalal nanachu nin

Oppamillathe mukhamippoleebhashayaaki

Tabor malamel soorya vavupol kandamukham

Bhavam pakarnnu mangichaavinte roopamaayi

Mullinmudi nin thalakkullill ninnozhukkeedum

Vellam polvarum rakthamullam thakarkkunayyo

Mohathil njangalkulla daahatthe nikkaan ninte

Dehathe baliyaakki snehatthekkattikondu

Swarlokamettil ninte nalloraattinkoottaththe

Ellam veetti dukhaabhadhikkallolkoottilvannu
