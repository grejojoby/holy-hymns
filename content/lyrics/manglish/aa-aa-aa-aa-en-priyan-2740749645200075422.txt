Aa aa aa aa... en priyan

Oo oo oo oo... nallavan

Iee vidha snehithan parilar?

Veendeduthon enne than chorayal;-

1 Lokamam kadal thira-thallalil

Pukumivan paarayin villalil

Kaividukayilla avan allalil

Veezhukil than shashwatha kaikalil;-

2 Chudezhunna shodana'velayil

Kude'vannu cheruma choolayil

Naswara'mallathoru dhehavum

Ie shareeram maatti than thannidum;-

3 Kuttinunde kudennum idhare

Veettiletthi vishramikum vare-

Hallelujah paadidum modhamai

Vallabhanam en priyan mulamai;
