Ennameru Paapathal bharamerum jeevitham

Enna vattiya vilakkumai neengidunna jeevitham

Veenudanja manpathramanu njan natha

Veendum oru jananam nalkidename natha

Karuna thonnane ennil alivu thonnane

Paapiyanu njan natha paapiyanu njan

Poorva paapathin shaapam peridunnu njan

Rogavum durithavum naalkkunaal valarunnu

Daivathin aathmavu ennil nirveeryamai

Paapam enne pathala vazhiyil ethichu

Karuna thonnane...

Ezhunalliduvan madichidalle Daivame

Snehavum karunayum ozhukkane natha

Pathiratti snehamode thirichu vannidan

Veendum enne vahikkane nin viricha chirakukalil

Ennamerum paapathal....
