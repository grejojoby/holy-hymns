Karthave kaniyaname

Mishihaye kaniyaname

Karthave njangalannakkum

Prarthana sadayam kelkkaname


Swargapithaavam sakalesha

Divyanugraha mekaname

Nara rakshakanam mishihaye

Divyanugraha mekaname...


Daivathmaavam sakalesha

Divyanugraham mekaname

Paripavanamam thrithwame

Divyanugraha mekaname


Bharanganam daivathin

Busura rashmikal pulkiduvaan

Deepamuyarthiya punyavathi

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Aadharapurvam savidhathil

Alphonsame njangalitha

Nilkukayayee nithyam nee

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Thee kanalpole erinjeedaan

Thee naalampol prabha thookaan

Mezhuthiri pole thlinjeedaan

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Paavananakum thirusuthanaaye

Pooja pushpam pole nee

Avananayaki hrudayasumam

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Papathin vazhi neekiduvaan

Punnyathin vazhi thedi nee

Keerthakayaayee lokathin

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Maanavennellam thaavakamam

Mathrukayennum Kaikolam

Papa kadangal neekukayaye

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Thyagam maathruka yakiya nee

Sneham kandathu sahanathil

Kudamaloorin Deepam nee

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Sanyasathin aaramam

Swantham aakanoru malarvaadi

Jeevitham oru poovanamaki

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Boudikam jeevitham akilamnee

Daivika sevanam aakiduvaan

Arpana maaki aathmam nee

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Parimala molum shyoonyasamam

Paarinu nalki sourabhyam

Preethan nathan kaikondu

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Lisyuvin cheru pushpampol

Laalichallo jeevithamam

Thoo malarenna vaadikayil

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Kerala bhoomikk abhimaanam

Paaram uyathiya punyavathi

Alphonsamme nithyam nee

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Lokam muzhuvanum abhikaamyam

Naga manohara maathruka nee

Sukrutham pakarum punyavathi

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay


Kanyaka mararee bhuvanathil

Kanthi pozhikkum nukathiraye

Preethi vallarthiya tharakame

Prarthikkaname njangalkkaay
