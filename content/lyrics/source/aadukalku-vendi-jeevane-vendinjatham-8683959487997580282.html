Aadukalku vendi jeevane vendinjatham
Devattin kuttiye! ninakanantha vannanam
Kadu neeleodi ada-loduzhannidum
Kunjadukal kabhayamam nin padam vandanam
Bheethi pokki adukalku munnadannu nee-
Sampreethiyai nadathidum krupakku vannanam
Pachame’chilum prasantha-thoyavum sada
Nee veezhchaye’nniye tharunnathorthu vannanam
Thatha puthranatmanam threeyeka deivame-
Sarvathmana ninakanantha keerthanam sadha
